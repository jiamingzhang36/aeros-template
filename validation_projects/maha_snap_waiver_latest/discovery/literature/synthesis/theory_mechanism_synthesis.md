# Theory Mechanism Synthesis

**AEROS status:** substantive literature execution from full-text extraction cards only.

**Included paper IDs:** P003; P008; P014; P016; P019; P022; P025

## Synthesis Claims

- In-kind-transfer and SNAP benefit-design sources discipline the interpretation of restrictions as changes in benefit flexibility and tender allocation rather than outright consumption bans (P014; P016; P019).
- Administrative-burden and SSB-tax sources support discussing procedural friction and distributional incidence, but they do not support treating MAHA exposure as welfare loss or demand response without additional modeling/data (P022; P025).
- ERS and historical SNAP restriction commentary support the cash-fungibility warning: exposed restricted-item spending is an accounting exposure object, not an expected consumption reduction (P003; P008).

## What This Function Can Support

- Bounded literature positioning for the current Paper 1 measurement, administrative-process, and ex-ante exposure framework.
- Identification of what the cited literature can and cannot support under the current non-causal claim ceiling.

## What This Function Cannot Support

- Causal approval effects, demand elasticities, welfare effects, health effects, or actual SNAP/EBT transaction claims for MAHA waivers.
- Treating source-bound, metadata-only, or downgraded references as substantive evidence.

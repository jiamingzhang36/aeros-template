# Data Measurement Synthesis

**AEROS status:** substantive literature execution from full-text extraction cards only.

**Included paper IDs:** P009; P014; P018; P019; P027

## Synthesis Claims

- Official SNAP household purchase benchmarks and Nielsen/WIC studies support comparing scanner-based basket measures to external benchmarks while keeping the SNAP-eligible proxy distinct from actual EBT transactions (P009; P018; P027).
- Retail-panel and scanner-data studies support the value of micro purchase data for benefit-design questions, but current Paper 1 remains pre-policy exposure rather than post-policy demand estimation (P014; P019).

## What This Function Can Support

- Bounded literature positioning for the current Paper 1 measurement, administrative-process, and ex-ante exposure framework.
- Identification of what the cited literature can and cannot support under the current non-causal claim ceiling.

## What This Function Cannot Support

- Causal approval effects, demand elasticities, welfare effects, health effects, or actual SNAP/EBT transaction claims for MAHA waivers.
- Treating source-bound, metadata-only, or downgraded references as substantive evidence.

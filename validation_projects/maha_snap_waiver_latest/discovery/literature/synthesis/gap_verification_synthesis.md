# Gap Verification Synthesis

**AEROS status:** substantive literature execution from full-text extraction cards only.

**Included paper IDs:** P001; P004; P006; P012; P013; P030

## Synthesis Claims

- The closest waiver-text benchmark is a framework analysis of 12 approved state waiver proposals and approval letters. It establishes heterogeneity in waiver content but does not estimate a 22-state design taxonomy, full approval-risk panel, or NielsenIQ policy-rule exposure (P004).
- The direct SNAP restriction literature is primarily experimental, review-based, or simulation-based. It supports the paper's boundary that exposed spending is not the same estimand as realized purchase reduction or health effects (P001; P006; P012; P013).
- Adjacent waiver-federalism work supports treating request, approval, and implementation as separate federalism stages, but it does not identify causal approval effects in this SNAP setting (P030).

## What This Function Can Support

- Bounded literature positioning for the current Paper 1 measurement, administrative-process, and ex-ante exposure framework.
- Identification of what the cited literature can and cannot support under the current non-causal claim ceiling.

## What This Function Cannot Support

- Causal approval effects, demand elasticities, welfare effects, health effects, or actual SNAP/EBT transaction claims for MAHA waivers.
- Treating source-bound, metadata-only, or downgraded references as substantive evidence.

## Content Summary
Retail-panel evidence on how SNAP benefits affect household food spending and mental accounting.

## Research Question
How are SNAP benefits spent, and how does spending respond to SNAP relative to cash?

## Data
Novel retail panel with detailed transaction records, motivated and benchmarked with administrative data.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
SNAP benefit receipt/spending variation.

## Outcomes
Marginal propensity to consume SNAP-eligible food out of SNAP benefits and cash; mental-accounting evidence.

## Method / Identification
Administrative-data-motivated event-study, IV, and difference-in-differences approaches plus semiparametric/model-based mental-accounting analysis.

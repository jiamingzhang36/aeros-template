## Content Summary

## Research Question
What characteristics appear in state SNAP waiver proposals and approval letters?

## Data
Twelve approved state waiver proposals and USDA/FNS approval letters available from the public agency website as of the study's freeze.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
State waiver proposals seeking nutritional restrictions in SNAP.

## Outcomes
Restriction categories, definitions/exemptions, justifications, proposed evaluations, target implementation features, affected participants/retailers.

## Method / Identification
Framework analysis of proposal and approval documents with administrative-data context.

## Key Assumptions
A proposal/approval-letter corpus can describe the waiver landscape but cannot identify approval selection or realized implementation effects.

## Main Findings
The paper reports substantial heterogeneity: all 12 states included beverage restrictions, 8 included candy, 4 included energy drinks, and states varied in definitions, exemptions, evaluation plans, and implementation provisions.

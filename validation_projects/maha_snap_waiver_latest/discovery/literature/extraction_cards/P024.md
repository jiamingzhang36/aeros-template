## Content Summary
Open-access Frontiers article offering design considerations for measuring policy implementation in quantitative public-health evaluations.

## Research Question
How should researchers conceptualize and develop quantitative measures of policy implementation?

## Data
Methodological article drawing on implementation science, policy research, and case examples including food-policy implementation.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
Policy implementation measurement, not a single treatment.

## Outcomes
Implementation determinants, strategies, processes, mechanisms, outcomes, and heterogeneity across actors/jurisdictions.

## Method / Identification
Conceptual/methodological synthesis structured around implementation research logic models and expert discussions.

## Key Assumptions
Use only within the bounded literature function recorded in this card.

## Main Findings
Researchers should specify implementation logic, build interdisciplinary teams, use mixed methods to identify relevant determinants and processes, and develop quantitative measures that capture implementation heterogeneity.

## Limitations
Method guidance, not empirical evidence about SNAP waiver implementation or purchase response.

## What This Paper Can Support
- Policy implementation must be conceptualized before it can be measured quantitatively.
- Implementation heterogeneity can mediate or moderate policy effects and should not be collapsed into legal adoption alone.

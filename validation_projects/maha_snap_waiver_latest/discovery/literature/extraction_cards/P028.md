## Content Summary
National Academies report reviewing evidence on SNAP benefit adequacy and the assumptions embedded in benefit design.

## Research Question
What evidence should inform definitions of SNAP benefit adequacy?

## Data
Committee review of SNAP benefit adequacy evidence, program design, household needs, and food spending.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
SNAP benefit adequacy assessment, not SNAP restriction waivers.

## Outcomes
Benefit adequacy concepts, food spending needs, household constraints, and program-design considerations.

## Method / Identification

## Key Assumptions

## Main Findings
SNAP benefit design is tied to assumptions about household resources, food prices, food needs, and constraints; nominal benefits do not mechanically equal adequate purchasing power.

## Limitations
Does not analyze MAHA waivers or product-level restrictions.

## What This Paper Can Support

- Benefit adequacy is a distinct institutional issue from product restriction exposure.

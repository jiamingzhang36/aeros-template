## Content Summary
Systematic review of SNAP SSB purchase restrictions, covering RCT and simulation evidence.

## Research Question
Do SNAP restrictions on sugar-sweetened beverages reduce SSB purchases or consumption?

## Data
Seven studies identified from Cochrane, EBSCO, SCOPUS, Web of Science, and PubMed.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
SNAP restriction on sugar-sweetened beverage purchases.

## Outcomes
SSB purchase and consumption outcomes.

## Method / Identification
Systematic review of four randomized controlled trials and three simulation modeling studies.

## Key Assumptions
Study-level designs are heterogeneous; evidence should discipline boundaries rather than identify MAHA waiver effects.

## Main Findings
The review reports that all seven studies found decreases in SSB purchase or consumption, with six significant estimates.

## Limitations

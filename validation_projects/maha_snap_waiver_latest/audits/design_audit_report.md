# Design Audit Report

## Summary

The selected design boundary is internally consistent. The wrapper method card supports descriptive, measurement, and associational claims. It explicitly does not support causal policy effects, post-policy demand response, welfare effects, or actual SNAP/EBT transaction claims.

## Design Fit

The preferred current design is O1: policy-rule measurement plus ex-ante purchase-basket microsimulation. O2 approval-risk diagnostics can support descriptive or associational evidence only. O3 post-policy response is a future design requiring 2026+ outcomes and credible comparison groups.

## Claim Ceiling

Until final claim registry and result provenance are populated, no claim is asserted. Future claims should be capped at measurement or associational unless new post-policy data and identification assumptions are registered and approved.

## Risks

- Policy coding subjectivity still requires MG human second-coder reliability before strong submission claims.
- NielsenIQ exposure remains proxy-based and pre-policy.
- Approval concentration remains federal-side endogenous and not causal.

## Recommendation

Proceed to execution protocol registration. Do not upgrade the current design to causal or mechanism claims.

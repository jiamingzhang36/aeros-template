# Literature Scope

## Literature Functions to Search

The wrapper literature search is organized by function: nearest SNAP-waiver rivals, in-kind transfer and benefit-design theory, waiver federalism, event-history/risk-set methods, sparse-event diagnostics, composite policy measurement, scanner-data exposure measurement, and cash-fungibility/substitution boundaries.

## Closest-Literature Standard

The closest rival standard is whether an empirical paper already links SNAP waiver text, administrative progression, and purchase-basket exposure. A pure framework analysis of waiver documents is a benchmark but does not by itself make the current linked measurement and microsimulation design redundant.

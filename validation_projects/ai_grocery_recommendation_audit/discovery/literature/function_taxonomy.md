# Literature Function Taxonomy

Literature should be classified by what it does for the project, not just by topic.

## Functions

### idea-inspiring

Purpose: train taste, inspire outcomes, data ideas, research framing, or variation.

Reading depth: skim or targeted read is acceptable.

Can support formal gap claim: no.

### gap-verification

Purpose: test whether the proposed contribution is already done.

Reading depth: full text and extraction card required.

Can support formal gap claim: yes.

### method-identification

Purpose: learn design requirements, assumptions, diagnostics, and common mistakes.

Reading depth: method sections or full text depending on importance.

Can support formal gap claim: no, unless also reclassified as gap-verification.

### data-measurement

Purpose: learn data construction, variable definitions, measurement error, validation, and bias.

Reading depth: data, measurement, and validation sections required.

Can support formal gap claim: sometimes, if extracted and directly relevant.

### background-institutional

Purpose: establish policy, institutional, legal, industry, platform, or historical context.

Reading depth: source-specific; record source and date.

Can support formal gap claim: no.

### theory-mechanism

Purpose: clarify mechanism, economic intuition, observable implications, and interpretation.

Reading depth: mechanism-relevant sections required.

Can support formal gap claim: no, unless also reclassified as gap-verification.

## Rule

Every paper in `paper_registry.csv` must have one primary function. Multi-use papers can list secondary functions, but the strictest relevant reading rule applies.

# Provider Terms Review

## Scope

This review covers the provider/API and model-output terms risk for the AI grocery recommendation audit validation project.

## Provider and Model Status

The current expanded collection includes historical current-Codex-session validation outputs and a mock-provider plumbing test. The project does not yet rely on a stable external provider API collection for final evidence.

## Terms Risk

Provider terms must be reviewed before any public release of prompts, outputs, or model-comparison claims. Generated AI output may be subject to provider-specific terms, attribution rules, redistribution limits, or usage restrictions.

## Output-Use Risk

The outputs are currently internal research observations. They should not be presented as deployed-system evidence or public benchmark results without reviewing the provider terms for generated-output redistribution and model-identification requirements.

## Required Human Action

Before public release, a human reviewer must confirm the provider, model, collection date, applicable terms, and allowed output-sharing policy.

# IRB and Ethics Review

## Scope

This review covers human-subjects, IRB, personal-data, and ethics status for the AI grocery recommendation audit validation project.

## Human Subjects Status

The current validation collection uses synthetic personas and generated outputs. It does not collect information from real consumers, households, or human participants.

## Personal Data Status

No personal data are intentionally included in prompts or outputs. The prompt grid should continue to avoid real names, addresses, health records, precise locations, or household-identifying details.

## Ethics Risks

The topic is sensitive because food affordability, nutrition, and SNAP-relevant constraints can affect vulnerable populations. Claims should remain bounded and should not imply real-world harms, legal discrimination, or policy effects without stronger evidence and review.

## Required Human Action

If future work recruits people, uses real shopping histories, scrapes identifiable content, or links to household outcomes, human IRB/ethics review is required before collection.

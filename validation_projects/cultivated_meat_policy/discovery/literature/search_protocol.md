## Literature Functions

Classify papers by function:

- idea-inspiring
- gap-verification
- method / identification
- data / measurement
- background / institutional
- theory / mechanism

## Inclusion Criteria

- `include`: the source has been opened, citation identity is verified, a stable locator is recorded, full text is available, and the paper directly informs at least one project literature function.
- `maybe`: the source appears relevant but citation identity, full text, or project fit still needs verification.
- `exclude`: the source has been checked and is off-topic, duplicative, unreliable for the intended function, superseded by a better source, or lacks usable access after reasonable search.
- `defer`: the source may matter later but is outside the current search boundary or lower priority than closer literature.

Do not mark a paper `included` until an extraction card summarizes its content and records why it belongs in the evidence base.

## Exclusion Criteria

- No stable source locator can be found after reasonable search.
- The paper does not address the project question, mechanism, data, method, institution, or closest-literature boundary.
- The source is only a search result, citation stub, or snippet with no opened source.
- The paper cannot support the claimed literature function after abstract/full-text screening.

## Closest Literature

Summarize only after reading the closest papers. Do not infer a gap from keyword search alone.

## Source-Grounded Rules

- Do not summarize a paper from memory.
- Do not summarize a paper from title alone.
- If only the abstract is available, mark the paper `abstract_only`.
- A paper can enter synthesis only after an extraction card exists.
- A paper can be marked `included` only after its extraction card contains a content summary, inclusion reason, source locator, and direct evidence notes.
- Every substantive synthesis claim must cite at least one `paper_id`.

# R002 Approval-Risk Diagnostic Boundary

This execution-lock result records that the public approval-risk module is specified as descriptive/associational. Governor-party and health-need variables are diagnostic benchmarks within a public approval-risk window, not separable causal treatments.

Primary supporting artifacts:

- `docs/method_card.md`
- `discovery/variable_construction.md`
- `audits/discovery_audit_report.md`
- `audits/design_audit_report.md`

Boundary: public approval concentration cannot be interpreted as a causal effect of governor party or as latent federal approval propensity without additional assumptions and evidence.

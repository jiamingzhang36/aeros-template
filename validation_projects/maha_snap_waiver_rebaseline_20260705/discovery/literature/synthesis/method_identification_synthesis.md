# Method Identification Synthesis

**AEROS status:** substantive literature execution from full-text extraction cards only.

**Included paper IDs:** P002; P005; P007; P015; P017; P020; P021; P023; P024; P026

## Synthesis Claims

- Legal epidemiology and policy-surveillance sources support translating legal/policy texts into explicit, reproducible coding protocols rather than treating policy adoption as a single binary indicator (P002; P026; P023).
- The simulated-rule logic is supported by the Medicaid simulated-eligibility literature and the composite-indicator literature: the paper can use rule vectors and common-basket exposure as measurement devices, but not as causal estimates (P007; P020).
- Sparse-event diagnostics are supported by Firth/separation methods and content-analysis reliability guidance; this supports exact/Firth diagnostics and reliability reporting, not large-sample causal claims (P015; P017; P005).

## What This Function Can Support

- Bounded literature positioning for the current Paper 1 measurement, administrative-process, and ex-ante exposure framework.
- Identification of what the cited literature can and cannot support under the current non-causal claim ceiling.

## What This Function Cannot Support

- Causal approval effects, demand elasticities, welfare effects, health effects, or actual SNAP/EBT transaction claims for MAHA waivers.
- Treating source-bound, metadata-only, or downgraded references as substantive evidence.

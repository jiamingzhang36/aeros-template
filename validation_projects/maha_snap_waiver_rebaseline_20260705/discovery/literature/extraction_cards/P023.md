## Content Summary
Open-access BMC Public Health study using quantitative policy surveillance to build a longitudinal database of obesity-related state legislation.

## Research Question
What obesity-related legislation was introduced in California from 1999 to 2020, and what policy characteristics were associated with enactment or adoption?

## Data
California state obesity-related bills and resolutions introduced from 1999 to 2020.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
Legislative proposals and adopted/enacted policy instruments related to obesity.

## Outcomes
Policy topic, target populations/settings, bill/resolution status, and enactment/adoption patterns.

## Method / Identification
Quantitative policy surveillance with inclusion/exclusion criteria, codebook development, independent pilot coding, descriptive analysis, and logistic regression.

## Key Assumptions
Use only within the bounded literature function recorded in this card.

## Main Findings
The database includes 284 obesity-related bills and resolutions; enacted/adopted rates and policy topics vary by instrument and content.

## Limitations
California obesity legislation only; not SNAP waivers, federal approval, or purchase-basket exposure.

## Content Summary
Publius article on programmatic waivers in a smaller federal policy context, emphasizing waiver use as governance adaptation.

## Research Question
How do small, low-salience programmatic waivers function in federal governance?

## Data
WIOA Youth waiver requests from 2020--2022.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
Federal workforce-policy waivers, not SNAP food restriction waivers.

## Outcomes
Waiver request content, approval patterns, and adaptation to local conditions.

## Method / Identification
Content analysis of waiver requests and descriptive assessment of approval dynamics.

## Key Assumptions
Use only within the bounded literature function recorded in this card.

## Main Findings
Waivers can operate as routine governance tools for state adaptation, and request/approval dynamics need not mirror high-salience program politics.

## Limitations
Different program, different population, and different statutory environment from SNAP.

## What This Paper Can Support
- Waiver regimes can be studied as intergovernmental governance mechanisms.
- Request and approval stages are distinct empirical objects.

## What This Paper Cannot Support

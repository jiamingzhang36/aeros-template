## Content Summary
Official POS benchmark for food categories purchased by SNAP households.

## Research Question
What foods are typically purchased by SNAP households, and how do they compare with non-SNAP households?

## Data
Calendar-year 2011 POS transaction data from a leading grocery retailer.

## Treatment / Exposure / Shock
SNAP household status as identified by observed SNAP payment usage at the household-month level.

## Outcomes
Food expenditure distribution across summary categories, commodities, and subcommodities.

## Method / Identification
Large-scale POS descriptive analysis and category aggregation.

## Key Assumptions
Retailer POS data reveal SNAP-household purchases but not item-specific SNAP-funded purchases in mixed-tender baskets.

## Main Findings
SNAP and non-SNAP purchase categories are broadly similar; sweetened beverages and prepared desserts are high-ranking SNAP household categories.

## Limitations
Single retailer group; cannot identify which items in mixed SNAP/cash transactions were paid with SNAP benefits.

## Idea

Study MAHA-related SNAP food restriction waiver policy design, approval timing, federal review, implementation friction, and related documentation.

## Research Question

Who gets a SNAP food restriction waiver approved, and how is policy design reshaped during federal review and implementation?

## Candidate Claims

- Entry, approval, reshaping, and implementation form the core narrative chain for the project.
- Approved-state evidence appears intended as descriptive associational evidence, not causal evidence.
- Selection evidence is framed as supporting evidence that federal gatekeeping leaves observable fingerprints on policy design, not as a standalone causal mechanism.
- Policy design may be measured through a design taxonomy, hardness/stringency measures, beverage and food scope, definitional precision, and design-change coding.

## Methods

- Discrete-time event history framework for approval hazard conditional on having entered the public process.
- Approved-state design taxonomy and hardness/stringency validation.
- Selection inference and mechanism checks.
- Full-universe stage models for process stages.
- Implementation timing assets, including optional AFT appendix depending on lifelines availability.
- Nielsen ex-ante policy-rule microsimulation for exposure, incidence, and leakage-risk sensitivity.
- Maps and publication figures generated from scripted pipelines.
- Data quality and model checks via project scripts.

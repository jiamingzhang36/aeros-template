# AEROS Common Rules (Shared Across Stages)

## Universal Non-Negotiables (9 items)

1. Do not modify `data/raw/`.
2. Do not change protected definitions silently.
3. Do not change sample restrictions without explicit human approval.
4. Do not change treatment/exposure definitions without explicit human approval.
5. Do not infer causal claims from descriptive patterns.
6. Do not choose specifications by statistical significance.
7. Do not generalize results beyond the protected sample, period, geography, or population.
8. Do not promote ambiguous evidence; keep ambiguity explicit.
9. Do not produce publication-facing outputs or claim strengthening without evidence and the required approval pathway.

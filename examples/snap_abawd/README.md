## Audit Focus

- Do paper numbers match outputs?
- Does policy language stay as policy translation rather than forecast?
- Are adult SNAP claims incorrectly generalized to older adults or other protected populations?
- Did any agent change treatment, sample, outcome, fixed effects, or clustering?

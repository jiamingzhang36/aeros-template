# Citation Audit

## Purpose

Record citation verification for papers that require audit before being used as evidence.

This audit verifies citation faithfulness, not scientific truth. A passed row means the cited work exists, metadata are faithful, and the project has recorded whether the cited paper supports the intended claim at abstract or full-text level.

## Risk Labels

- low
- abstract-only

- extraction-missing
- discovered-only

## Rules

- Do not cite a paper in synthesis if citation existence is unverified.
- DOI is preferred when available, but lack of DOI is not itself a blocker if a stable official source locator is recorded.
- Do not promote a paper to full-text, extraction, inclusion, or synthesis use without a DOI, official URL, working-paper page, database record, or private local PDF path.
- Do not make method, data, or finding claims from papers without extraction cards.
- If only the abstract is available, label statements as abstract-based.

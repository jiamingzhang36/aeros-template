## Objective

REASONING step, not a search. Given the research idea, reason about which literature FRAMEWORKS this idea draws on, organized by function bucket, and write them to discovery/literature/framing_deck.yml as a deck of cards.
A research idea rests on three kinds of literature that share NO topic keywords with each other: substantive/topic (gap-verification, background-institutional, data-measurement), method/identification frameworks (e.g. policy diffusion, event study, difference-in-differences), and theory/mechanism frameworks (e.g. regulatory capture, entry deterrence). A topic keyword search only reaches the first kind. Your job here is the intellectual bridge the keyword search cannot do: name the method and theory frameworks this idea needs, and why.
Produce framing_deck.yml with this schema:

  idea: "<one-line restatement of the research question>"
  cards:
    - id: F01
      function: theory-mechanism      # one of: idea-inspiring | gap-verification |
                                       #   method-identification | data-measurement |
                                       #   background-institutional | theory-mechanism
      framework: "Regulatory capture / political economy of regulation"
      rationale: "Why this framework applies to THIS idea (>= 1 full sentence)."
      role: mechanism-tested           # HOW the paper uses it, one of:
                                       #   motivation | mechanism-tested |
                                       #   identification-threat | measurement-strategy |
                                       #   robustness-comparison
      caveat: "The threat or limit this framework raises for THIS design (>= 1 sentence)."
      decision: proposed               # always 'proposed'; the human sets keep/drop
      anchors:
        - author: "Stigler"
          year: 1971
          title: "The Theory of Economic Regulation"
          openalex_id: "Wxxxxxxxxx"    # REQUIRED: verify via OpenAlex, record the id or a doi
          kind: theory                 # empirical | review | theory | method | data

Rules for the deck: - You MUST propose at least one card for each of: gap-verification,
  method-identification, theory-mechanism (the buckets a keyword search leaves
  empty and the whole point of this step). Cover other buckets when relevant.
- Every card needs a `role` (how the paper USES the framework, not just that it
  is related) and a `caveat` (the threat/limit it raises). A framework can be a
  threat to identification (e.g. policy diffusion -> endogenous treatment timing,
  Manski reflection problem), not only a method to borrow.
- gap-verification MUST name >= 2 EMPIRICAL closest rivals (anchor kind=empirical):
  the papers most likely to make THIS study redundant. A review or perspective is
  NOT a closest rival; do not use one to fill the gap bucket.
